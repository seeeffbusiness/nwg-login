Privacy Policy

NWG Moderation Tools · Effective October 21, 2026 (updated for time zones) · Terms of Use

This policy explains what information NWG Moderation Tools (“the site”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, how it is protected, and the choices you have. The site is an internal moderation tool run by Seef, Staff & Community Manager, on behalf of Nova Wave Games (“NWG”), the studio behind the Roblox experiences Saber Showdown and Saber Legends. It is used only by approved NWG moderation staff and a small number of view-only guests.

Questions or requests: seeeffbusiness@gmail.com.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Staff users: NWG team members who log in with Discord and Roblox.
  • Guest users: people given a view-only username and password by the site owner.
  • Players and members in moderation records: Roblox players and Discord members who appear in NWG's ban records or staff moderation logs. They don't use the site, but the site stores information about them (see section 3).

2. Information we collect about staff users

From Discord, when you log in

  • Your Discord user ID, username, and profile picture link.
  • Whether you're a member of the NWG staff Discord server and whether you have its NWG Tools Access role. We only check for that role; we don't store your other roles or servers.
  • A Discord login token, which lets the site re-check that role about every 30 minutes while you use it. It is stored encrypted and deleted when your session ends or your account is removed.

We ask Discord only for the identify and guilds.members.read permissions. The site can't read your messages or your email address, and it can't post, join servers or act as you.

From Roblox, when you log in

  • Your Roblox user ID, username, and profile picture link (Roblox's openid and profile permissions).
  • Your rank in the Nova Wave Games Roblox group, read from Roblox's public group information.

We don't keep a Roblox login token after the login finishes. The site can't change anything on your Roblox account.

That you give us

  • The display name you choose.
  • An optional profile picture you upload. It is cropped and resized in your browser to 512×512 and stored as an image file.

Created as you use the site

  • Account details: when you signed up, your approval status and role (Moderator, Senior Mod, Admin or Owner), and when you last changed your name.
  • Session records: an encrypted (hashed) copy of your login token, its expiry, and when your access was last re-checked.
  • Presence: while the site is open and visible, your browser checks in about once a minute so staff can see who is online. The check-in itself only updates the time you were last seen.
  • Site activity: what you do on the site, sent with your check-ins about every 5 minutes: how many minutes you were actively using it, which pages and tabs you opened, what you searched for (ban search, evader search, log search), which players you looked up (all bans, name history, alts, logs about a player), whose moderation logs you opened, and what you copied (for example an ID, a username, a Legends command or a batch of IDs). Each item has the time it happened. We don't record clicks, typing, mouse or scroll movement, your settings, or chat messages. Only Admins and the Owner can see it.
  • Moderation log statistics: from your moderation logs, the site counts how many you post each week (Monday to Sunday, Mountain Time). Your weekly count and place appear on a leaderboard every staff member with access to the logs can see. Admins and the Owner also see a breakdown: weekly counts and averages, when you last logged, the types of logs, how many have evidence, how many the site couldn't read, and your busiest days and hours.
  • Activity log: logins, sign-ups, account changes, uploads of ban files and other admin actions, with your display name and your Discord and Roblox usernames.
  • Staff chat: messages you send in the site's staff chat, with your display name, picture and the time sent. Everyone logged in to the site can read them. Messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Your moderation logs: if you post moderation logs in the staff Discord's log forums, the site shows them under your name (see section 3).
  • Time zone: the time zone your browser is set to (for example “Europe/London”), saved when you use the site and updated if it changes. Your weekly log counts and busiest times are worked out in it, and it’s shown to all staff on your log page and in staff activity, with your current local time. We don’t store your location or IP address for this.
  • Staff list: about once an hour, the site's Discord bot reads the member list of the NWG staff Discord server (user ID, username, server nickname and profile picture link, and whether you have the Retired Management role) to know who is current staff. It doesn't store anyone's other roles.

3. Information about players in moderation records

To enforce the rules of NWG's games, the site stores moderation records exported from NWG's own Roblox moderation tools: the player's Roblox user ID, username, ban date, ban status and length, public and private ban reasons, and whether alternate accounts were banned. To help moderators recognize returning players, it also stores usernames and past usernames looked up from Roblox's public user information.

Discord moderation logs (beta). A bot on NWG's own Discord app reads two log forums in the NWG staff Discord server (in-game logs and Discord logs) and copies each log into the site so staff can search it. For each log the site stores: the text of the log message (including the player's Roblox username, or the member's Discord username and Discord user ID, the reason, the punishment and its length), the staff member who posted it, when it was posted or edited, and the names and links of any attached evidence. Evidence images and videos themselves are not copied; they're shown straight from Discord and stop showing if they're deleted there. When a log is edited or deleted in Discord, the site updates or removes its copy. The bot can only read the channels it's given access to; management channels aren't read.

These records are visible only to approved staff (moderation logs) and view-only guests (ban records only). They aren't published, sold or shared outside NWG. Players with questions about a ban should use NWG's usual appeal process, or email the address above.

4. Information about guest users

For view-only guests we store the username, a salted, stretched hash of the password (never the password itself), when the account was created, the last login time, failed login attempts (to lock out guessing), and session records like those above. Guest logins appear in the activity log.

5. How we use information

  • To decide who may use the site: checking the Discord role, the Roblox group rank, and owner approval, at every login and again about every 30 minutes.
  • To show staff who is online, and each staff member's display name and picture.
  • To keep a record of changes, so the team stays accountable and can investigate misuse.
  • To support NWG's moderation of its games.
  • To let Admins and the Owner see how staff use the site and how active they are with moderation logs, so the team stays accountable and the workload is fair.

We don't use any of this for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone. The activity and log statistics above are only for running the staff team.

6. Services that handle information for us

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database. Information is stored and processed on Cloudflare's network, which may be outside your country.
  • Discord and Roblox handle the logins under their own privacy policies. Discord also hosts the moderation logs and their evidence, which the site reads through its bot.
  • GitHub Pages hosts a one-line forwarding page that Roblox logins pass through on the way back to the site. GitHub may log that visit, as any website does, but the one-time login code it carries can't be used by anyone else.
  • Profile pictures that come from Discord or Roblox load straight from their image servers, which see your browser's request.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of NWG's players and staff.

7. Cookies and browser storage

  • nwg_s: keeps staff logged in, for up to 7 days.
  • nwg_login: protects the login process from tampering; lasts 10 minutes.
  • nwg_guest: keeps guests logged in on the guest site, for up to 7 days.

These are essential cookies. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. The site also saves moderation data and your display settings in your browser, so pages load faster and your preferences are remembered. Logging out deletes the saved moderation data. You can also clear it any time in Settings, or by clearing your browser's site data.

8. How long we keep information

  • Staff accounts (names, IDs, pictures): while you are on the staff team. Removed when the owner removes your account or you ask us to.
  • Sessions: until you log out, they expire after 7 days, or your access check fails. Expired sessions are cleaned up automatically.
  • Login records: about 10 minutes.
  • Activity log: kept for staff accountability while the site is in use, and for no more than 2 years.
  • Moderation records: as long as NWG needs them to enforce its rules.
  • Discord moderation logs: as long as the original log stays in the NWG staff Discord server. Deleting it there removes it from the site.
  • Staff chat messages: 24 hours, then deleted automatically.
  • Site activity: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Time zone: with your account; replaced when it changes, and removed with your account.
  • Staff list: replaced each time the bot reads it; someone who leaves the staff server drops off at the next read.
  • Moderation log statistics: worked out from the logs whenever the page is opened, not stored separately.

9. How we protect information

The site only works over HTTPS. Login tokens are stored hashed, and Discord tokens encrypted. Guest passwords are randomly generated and stored only as salted hashes, and accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords. Every login requires both a Discord and a Roblox account and owner approval, and access is re-checked regularly. The owner can lock everyone out at once in an emergency. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

10. Your choices and rights

You can change your display name (once every 12 hours) and your picture in Settings, and log out at any time. To see the information we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, email us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the use of your information, and to complain to a data protection authority. We'll answer requests within 30 days. Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure moderation tool for NWG's games.

11. Age

Staff accounts require Discord and Roblox accounts, so you must meet their minimum age (13 in most countries). If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian knows you use this tool. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13 through the site. If you think that has happened, email us and we'll delete it.
